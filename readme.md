## ◈・Overview

**The Fulda Crossing: 1983** is a custom server project within SCP: Roleplay, built around an original fictional setting inspired by the atmosphere and military aesthetics of the Cold War.

The project features its own lore, setting, and gameplay experience, developed and managed by its designated development team.

Rather than being a standalone game, it is a custom experience built within an existing Roblox game.

## ◈・The Setting

The year is 1983.

Drawing inspiration from the geopolitical tensions, military aesthetics, and atmosphere of the Cold War, the project establishes its own fictional setting and scenarios.

Neither side is inherently represented as the favored side of the project. Both are part of the setting, and their respective roles are determined by the project's fictional narrative and gameplay.

> **Disclaimer:** The Fulda Crossing: 1983 is a fictional project and is not intended to depict actual historical events. Any similarities to real-world individuals, organizations, locations, or events are coincidental or incorporated within a fictional context.
